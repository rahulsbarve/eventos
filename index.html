How EventOS uses WhatsApp.

EventOS works on the channels a venue already uses: WhatsApp, phone and email. For WhatsApp, it uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta.

Read how we handle personal data in our privacy policy.

  • Venues connect their own numbers.

    A venue links its WhatsApp Business numbers to EventOS through Meta's own sign-up flow, with its permission, and can disconnect them at any time.

  • Families start the conversation.

    EventOS handles the messages families send to the venue. The first reply includes a short note on how their details are used, with a link to our privacy policy.

  • A person at the venue approves every reply.

    EventOS reads each enquiry and drafts a reply. Someone at the venue checks it before it is sent. The only automatic messages are ones the venue writes and switches on, such as an after-hours acknowledgement.

  • Follow-ups and updates use approved templates.

    Reminders, confirmations and receipts sent outside an open conversation use message templates approved by Meta, sent from the venue's own number.

  • A team assistant for venue staff.

    Venue team members get their morning brief and ask questions on WhatsApp through an EventOS assistant number.

  • No marketing of our own.

    We don't use families' numbers for our own marketing, and we never sell personal data.
