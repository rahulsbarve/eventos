This policy explains what personal data EventOS collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

1Who we are

EventOS is run by SmrtEvent Gatherings Private Limited ("EventOS", "we", "us"), a company registered in India, CIN U62099KA2026PTC223945, with its registered office at Unit 101, Oxford Towers, 139/88 HAL Old Airport Rd, H.A.L II Stage, Bangalore North, Bangalore 560008, Karnataka, India.

For anything about your personal data, write to our Grievance Officer at rishi@myeventos.in.

2Who this policy covers

  • Website visitors: anyone who visits myeventos.in or contacts us through it.
  • Venues and their teams: venues that use EventOS, and the people at those venues who use it.
  • Families and guests: people who enquire about or book an event at a venue that uses EventOS.
  • Wedding businesses: photographers, caterers and other wedding businesses that contact us or work with us.

3What we collect

  • From website visitors: what you send us when you write, call or message us, such as your name, business or venue name, WhatsApp number, email and message. Like most websites, our web host keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and running.
  • From venues and their teams: account and contact details, the WhatsApp numbers linked to EventOS, and the information your team enters or sends through EventOS.
  • From families and guests: the details shared with a venue when you enquire or book, such as your name, contact details, event date, guest count, preferences, messages and voice notes, and payment records the venue enters. We do not collect card or bank details.
  • From wedding businesses: what you share with us, such as your business details, portfolio link and contact details.

If you link your own WhatsApp number: we capture only your conversations with customers about enquiries and bookings. When you link it, we import only live enquiries and bookings from your chat history. Chats with colleagues and personal chats are dropped as they arrive and never stored.

4Our role with your data

For website visitors, venue teams and wedding businesses, we decide how your data is used.

For families and guests, we mostly process your data on behalf of the venue you're dealing with. The venue decides what is collected and how it's used for your event.

5Why we use it

  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To provide EventOS to venues: capturing enquiries, drafting replies, managing bookings and helping coordinate events.
  • To read enquiries and draft replies with AI. A person at the venue checks every reply before it is sent.
  • To keep EventOS secure, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

6Consent

We ask for your consent before using your personal data. For families, the venue asks for consent when you first enquire, and the first reply you receive includes a short note on how your data is used, with a link to this policy.

You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to rishi@myeventos.in. This doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew it.

7Who we share it with

  • The venue you've enquired at or booked with.
  • Companies that help us run EventOS, listed below. They may use your data only to provide their service to us, and we update this list when it changes.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

8Where we store it

Your data is stored in India and encrypted, both in transit and at rest. Some messages are processed briefly outside India where WhatsApp or the AI models we use require it; nothing is stored there.

9How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

When you ask us to delete your data, we delete your name, contact details and private information such as ID numbers. We keep records about the event itself, such as dates, bookings and payments, where the venue or we need them for accounting or legal reasons.

10Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it, by withdrawing consent;
  • let someone else act for you if you die or can no longer act yourself.

Write to rishi@myeventos.in. We'll check that the request comes from you, and respond within 30 days. If your data was collected by a venue, we may pass your request to the venue and help it respond.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Email rishi@myeventos.in with the subject "Delete my data", or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Tell us the phone number or email address your data is linked to and, if you're a family or guest, the venue you dealt with.
  3. We'll check that the request comes from you, delete your data as described in section 9, and confirm within 30 days.

If you're a venue, your admin can also export your data and ask us to close your account; we delete it within 30 days of closing.

12How we protect it

Your data is encrypted and protected so that only authorised people can see it. Our team looks at it only to set up and support the service, and every action in EventOS is recorded. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

13Children

Our website and services are not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data without a parent's or guardian's consent. A venue may add children to an event's guest list; those details are used only to run that event.

14Cookies and analytics

Our website doesn't set cookies, use analytics or load anything from other companies' servers.

15Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
